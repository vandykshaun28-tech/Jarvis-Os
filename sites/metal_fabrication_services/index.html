About Shaun Van Dyk

With a career spanning over two decades, Shaun Van Dyk brings a wealth of experience in metal fabrication, honed from working for major suppliers to the automotive industry in South Africa. Metal Fabrication Services

We fabricate custom parts, such as brackets made from 4130 steel alloy, and provide repair services for machinery, including the maintenance of hydraulic cylinders at our workshop in Johannesburg.

Projects and Capabilities

At metal_fabrication_services, we specialize in creating precision-fabricated parts for demanding industrial applications, utilizing CNC machining centers and 3D metal printing to produce parts from materials such as aluminum, steel, and titanium. Our expertise includes designing and manufacturing components for the aerospace and defense industries, as well as developing custom tooling and fixtures for various manufacturing processes. From prototyping to production, we bring Shaun Van Dyk's meticulous attention to detail to every project.
